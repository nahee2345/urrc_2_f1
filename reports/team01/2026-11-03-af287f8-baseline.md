## Physical and feature acceptance gates
- **Physics/start:** build, SDF, world spawn, five live sensor topics, full-red→lights-out camera gate, adapter and Gazebo odometry are actually observed.
- **Lap 1:** independent `GEOMETRIC_LAP_PASS` plus camera/LiDAR validity, track clearance, and sustained movement; do not treat commanded speed integral as traveled distance.
- **SLAM:** inspect `/tf` chain, `team01_map -> base_link` freshness, recorded map points and RTAB-Map loop-closure evidence; a saved JSON file is not enough.
- **Lap 2 (follow-on scenario; NOT covered by the single-lap CI):** map-ready transition only after true closure; record actual odometry speed, not command speed, then test progressive 3/5/8/10m/s, smooth steering and no offtrack.
- **Obstacle scenarios (follow-on):** static wall vs curve, stationary and moving car, pass → merge, and stale rear LiDAR. Verify a rejoin does not enter an unverified corridor.
- **Performance:** plot measured steering and actual speed against command, lateral deviation, steering oscillation, sensor age and verified forward distance. Do not claim "10m/s stable" until real physical second-lap proof.
